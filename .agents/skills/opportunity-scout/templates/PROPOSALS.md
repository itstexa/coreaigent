# Proposals Register

Candidate scope surfaced by [opportunity-scout](../../.github/skills/opportunity-scout/SKILL.md). Nothing here is approved or committed — a proposal has no authority until a human operator promotes it into [docs/design/DESIGN.md](./design/DESIGN.md) as a user story.

Two classes:

- **`PP-` Refinement** — a grounded gap, anchored to concrete evidence.
- **`PD-` Direction** — opinionated product vision, licensed to challenge whether approved scope is aimed at the right thing.

Rows are never deleted. A `Declined` proposal is a record of a decision and stops the same idea being re-proposed.
